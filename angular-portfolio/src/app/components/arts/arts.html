Arts & Culture

Where logic meets imagination — my life around the campus cultural scene.

I've always been drawn to art in every form. Drawing is my favorite way to slow down and express what code cannot say. At ESTIN, I never miss an opportunity to contribute to the cultural and artistic events organized on campus: the Opening Day competition and the scientific club exhibition in 2025, a drawing for Yennayer, Art Week in April 2026, and most recently the Octobre Rose exhibition, where I displayed my own drawings.
